BASEMENTAL FG 250 FOR LAVA STUDIO (lavaCloud)
=============================================

Upload to https://cloud.lavamusic.com (log in with your LAVA account):

  1. NAM A2-Lite        10 amp captures. UPLOAD THESE FIRST: small (44 KB) and made
                        for LAVA STUDIO's A2-Lite engine.
  2. IR 48 kHz          24 cabinet IRs (.wav, mono, 48 kHz, 24-bit).
  3. NAM A2 (Full+Lite) The same 10 captures with both sizes in one file. Only needed
                        if the Lite files are refused.

On LAVA STUDIO:
  - NAM: add the files from lavaCloud to STUDIO's local NAM library, then in a preset:
    AMP slot > NAM tab > a NAM Loader > pick the capture.
  - IR:  add the files from lavaCloud to STUDIO's local IR folder, then in the preset:
    CAB slot > IR Loader > pick the IR.

The captures are the amp head only: always pair one with an IR.
Good first pairs:
  FG250_Clean_Glassy_Lite         + FG250_IR_01_Fusion_1x12_OnAxis_48k
  FG250_Clean_Jazz_Neck_Lite      + FG250_IR_07_1x12_Jazz_Combo_OnAxis_48k
  FG250_Lead_Fusion_Crunch_Lite   + FG250_IR_03_2x12_Closed_Back_OnAxis_48k
  FG250_Lead_Sweep_Lite           + FG250_IR_01_Fusion_1x12_OnAxis_48k
  FG250_Lead_Fat_Round_Neck_Lite  + FG250_IR_04_4x12_Closed_Back_Close_48k
