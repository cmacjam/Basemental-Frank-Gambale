Basemental FG 250 is installed

Logic Pro: open Logic, click an Audio FX slot on a guitar track and choose Audio Units > Basemental > Basemental FG 250.

If it isn't listed, go to Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager, select Basemental FG 250 and click Reset & Rescan Selection.

Click TUNER in the plug-in for the strobe tuner, and try the Gambale presets in the top-right menu.
